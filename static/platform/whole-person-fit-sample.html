How is fit measured?

Your answers are compared to what people in each job typically report. We look at six areas: your interests (what you like to do), work values (what matters to you at work), knowledge (what you know), skills (what you can do), and personality (how you tend to think and act).

Each area gets a score from 0–100% showing how similar you are to people in that job. Your overall fit is the average of those six scores.

How does fit predict career outcomes?

Research shows that when your profile matches a job well, you are more likely to enjoy it, stay longer, and feel that the job meets your needs. The "Career Outcomes" section shows predicted scores for things like job satisfaction and fit—based on how well your profile aligns with the role.
