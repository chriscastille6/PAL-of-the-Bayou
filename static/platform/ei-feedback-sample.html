About This Assessment

Emotional intelligence is the ability to recognize, understand, regulate, and use emotions in yourself and others. This assessment uses situational judgment and related tasks to measure the four facets above. Research links EI to job performance, leadership, and well-being (e.g., O'Boyle et al., 2011).

Mayer, J. D., & Salovey, P. (1997). What is emotional intelligence? In P. Salovey & D. Sluyter (Eds.), Emotional development and emotional intelligence (pp. 3–31). Basic Books. O'Boyle, E. H., et al. (2011). The relation between emotional intelligence and job performance: A meta-analysis. Journal of Organizational Behavior, 32(5), 788–818.
