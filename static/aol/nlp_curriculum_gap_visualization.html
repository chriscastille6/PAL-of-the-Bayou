Curriculum Alignment & Assurance of Learning

Validating NLP & LLM Approaches for KSAO Profiling in Business Education

Adopting an NLP or LLM-based approach to map degree plans against occupational domains is highly feasible. Evidence demonstrates that machine learning models can accurately parse text to estimate Knowledge, Skills, Abilities, and Other characteristics (KSAOs). Applying this to Business College curricula (e.g., HR, Marketing) enables precise gap analysis between academic offerings and real-world occupational requirements.

Empirical Foundation: Putka et al. (2023)

The feasibility of your approach is anchored in recent psychometric research. Putka et al. (2023) demonstrated that NLP models can process job text to generate KSAO ratings that closely mirror human Subject Matter Expert (SME) ratings.
