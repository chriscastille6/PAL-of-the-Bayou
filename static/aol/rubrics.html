Terminology: direct vs. indirect measures

Direct measures
Scored evidence of learning — in practice, the rubrics faculty use to rate student work on program outcomes.
Indirect measures
Surveys (senior, alumni, employer, etc.). They inform improvement; they do not replace rubrics or exams.
