### What is this study about?

This study examines how students and HR professionals evaluate the effectiveness of HR management tactics in realistic workplace scenarios. Your responses will help us understand patterns in HR decision-making and improve evidence-based HR education. Results will be reported only in aggregate or de-identified form.

### What would you do?

1. Open the survey hub and select the **class decision** you are preparing for (Decision 1–8).  
2. Read the informed consent and decide whether to participate.  
3. Enter your first and last name to generate a **Candidate ID** (your name is not stored with research data). Use the **same name** for every pack so your surveys can be linked.  
4. Complete a brief **optional demographics** questionnaire (you may skip any item).  
5. Rate HR tactics on a 1–5 scale (or skip any item).  
6. Download your **PDF report** and submit it as directed for class (e.g., Canvas).

**Hub link:** https://bayoupal.nicholls.edu/hr-sjt-assessment/modular-surveys/

**Time:** About 5–15 minutes per decision pack (up to 8 packs across the term).

### Is this voluntary?

**Yes.** Research participation is **VOLUNTARY**. You may skip any rating, skip any demographic question, skip a pack, or withdraw at any time by closing the browser without penalty. You must be **18 years of age or older** to participate in the research study.

Course requirements for submitting a PDF (if any) are separate from research consent. Declining research use of your responses will not affect your standing in the course beyond ordinary assignment policies announced by your instructor.

### Risks and benefits

Risks are minimal (time; feedback may differ from your expectations). Benefits may include practice with HR situational judgment, a PDF summary of your ratings, and contribution to research on HR education. There is no monetary payment.
