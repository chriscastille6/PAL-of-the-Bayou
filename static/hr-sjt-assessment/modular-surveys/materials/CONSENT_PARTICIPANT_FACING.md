## Study header (always)

**Research Study:** HR Situational Judgment Test – Evidence-Based HR Decision-Making  

**Principal Investigator:** Dr. Christopher M. Castille  
**Institution:** Nicholls State University  

---

## Purpose

This research examines how students and working professionals evaluate the effectiveness of HR management tactics in situational judgment scenarios. Your responses will be used for research and teaching in aggregate. No names are stored with research data—only a participant ID.

---

## What you will do (this sitting)

You will complete **one short survey pack** covering the decision topic shown on screen ([pack-specific topic inserted]). You may:

1. Complete a brief **optional demographics** questionnaire (you may skip any item; asked once per Candidate ID).  
2. Rate tactics on a 1–5 scale or **skip** any item.  
3. Download a PDF report. Students may upload the PDF to Canvas as directed by the instructor.

There are up to **8 packs** across the study; the same name always produces the same Candidate ID so your packs can be linked.

---

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any demographic item, skip any rating, skip this pack, or withdraw at any time by closing the browser. Contact the investigator to request destruction of data already collected.

You must be **18 years of age or older**.

---

## Risks and benefits

**Risks:** Minimal (time commitment; feedback or aggregate results may differ from your expectations).  

**Benefits:** Practice with HR situational judgment; a PDF summary of your ratings; contribution to research on HR education. Students may receive course credit as determined by the instructor. There is no monetary payment.

---

## Confidentiality

Your name is used only to generate a participant ID and is not stored with research data. Demographic answers (if provided) are stored with your participant ID only. Report the ID on your PDF for course upload if required. Data are reported only in aggregate or de-identified form.
