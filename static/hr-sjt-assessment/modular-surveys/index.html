About this exercise

This survey supports an HR Mentoring Program we are building with local HR professionals. You share how you would handle each case; the mentors you partner with share their ratings as well. Together, the exercise is a knowledge-sharing conversation—not a test with one correct answer.

In class, we sample both student and mentor perspectives so the group can see how people view the same situation. We also discuss relevant evidence from the research literature (for example through Consensus.app and Elicit) to inform decision-making. That is an evidence-based management approach: act on the best available information while remaining willing to doubt what we believe to be true, as Pfeffer reminds us.

Use the same first and last name on every survey so your Candidate ID links across decisions. Demographics are optional and never required to finish a pack.
