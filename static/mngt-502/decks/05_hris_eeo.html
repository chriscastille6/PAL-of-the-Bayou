LO 3.2

Visualizations should be sensegiving

Sensegiving orients interpretation toward a decision (Patriotta, 2017; Knaflic, 2015).

What decision does this chart change — or is it decoration?

  • What decision does this inform?
  • Who must act differently after seeing it?
  • What data quality would make you trust it?

Sensegiving

Sensegiving amounts to tugging readers down a predetermined path and orienting their interpretation in a preferred direction (Patriotta, 2017).

Sensegiving steers interpretation along a preferred path — not neutral decoration.

Framing the pitch
