# Student explainer — Year-1 HCROI Midpoint Check-in

**Course:** MNGT 502 Managing Human Capital  
**When:** After you finish Year-1 of the Interpretive HRManagement simulation (quarters Q1–Q4), before or during your midpoint meeting with Dr. Castille.  
**Live tool:** https://bayou-pal.netlify.app/mngt-502/year1-hcroi-checkin/

## What you will do

1. Skim the refresher on **HCCF** (pay & benefits, contingents, absenteeism $, turnover $) and **HCROI**.
2. Enter your Year-1 workforce and cost figures (or start from the demo, then replace with your team’s numbers).
3. Read the computed **rates** and **dollar costs by bucket**.
4. Click **Open ROI calculator with Year-1 totals** to see HCRF / HCCF / HCVA / HCROI.
5. **Print / Save PDF** of the one-page brief for the meeting.

## What this is (and is not)

- It is a **teachable worksheet** that connects sim play to Fitz-enz language.
- It is **not** the Interpretive simulation engine and not a graded auto-submit form.
- **Nothing is uploaded or stored** on the site. Your team name stays in the browser for printing only.

## Key formulas you should recognize

- **Turnover rate** = separations ÷ average workforce  
- **Absence rate** = days lost ÷ (workforce × workable days)  
- **HCCF** = pay & benefits + contingents + absenteeism $ + turnover $  
- **HCROI** = (Revenue − (Expenses − HCCF)) / HCCF  

Turnover $ uses four buckets: Separation · Replacement · Training · Performance.  
Absenteeism $ uses four buckets: Absentees · Managing absence · Substitutes · Reduced quantity/quality.
