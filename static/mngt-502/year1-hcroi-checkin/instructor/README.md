# MNGT 502 — Year-1 HCROI Midpoint Check-in (Instructor)

**Pedagogical moment:** Midpoint meeting with the instructor **after Year-1** of the Interpretive HRManagement simulation (≈ Q1–Q4). This is **not** a Year-3 / Q9–Q12 tool.

**Canonical student URL:** https://bayou-pal.netlify.app/mngt-502/year1-hcroi-checkin/

**Related live tool:** https://bayou-pal.netlify.app/platform/tools/roi-calculator/

## Purpose

Students refresh Fitz-enz / Cascio–Boudreau HCROI frames, turn Year-1 sim figures into rates and dollar costs (HCCF building blocks), bridge into the ROI calculator, and bring a one-page brief to the midpoint check-in.

## What to ask students to bring from Interpretive

Typical Year-1 exports / reports / worksheets:

- Starting and ending headcount (or quarterly headcount to average)
- Separations / turnover counts or rates for Q1–Q4 (convert rates to counts if needed)
- Absence days or hours lost
- Payroll + benefits totals (or wages × benefits %)
- Contingent / temporary labor spend and FTEs if reported
- Revenue and total expenses if available from financial reports

See `Field_Map.md` for field → tool input mapping.

## Facilitation notes (10–15 minutes)

1. Confirm they used **their** Year-1 numbers (demo data is for exploration only).
2. Ask which HCCF component dominates and whether that matches their Year-1 experience.
3. Probe one Year-2 decision that would move turnover or absence $ without killing productivity.
4. Optional: open the bridged ROI calculator and glance at HCROI vs classroom Bayou Energy defaults (~1.63).

## Admin / grading (light)

- Completion check: printed or PDF brief with team name + sensible rates/$ (not a precision audit).
- Rubric can be binary (brief present + two discussion answers) or 0–2 points for interpretation quality.
- Do **not** require students to upload data to Netlify — the tool is client-side only.

## FERPA / privacy

- No student PII or education records are collected or stored on bayou-pal.netlify.app for this tool.
- Team name is local-only in the browser for print.
- If a brief is submitted, use Canvas (or your LMS) — not this site.

## Out of scope

Full Cascio 12-step micro-UI; Y3 Budget Builder; Shiny HC costing redeploy; Program ROI / NPV (optional link only to `/ua/home/`).
