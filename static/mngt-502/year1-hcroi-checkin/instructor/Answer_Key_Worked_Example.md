## Expected rates

| Metric | Formula | Approx. result |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Avg workforce | (680 + 700) / 2 | **690.0** |
| Turnover rate | 56 / 690 | **≈ 8.1%** |
| Absence rate | 2,380 / (690 × 250) | **≈ 1.4%** |

## Teaching point

Demo Year-1 HCCF is still dominated by **pay & benefits**, but students should still see that **turnover + absence** are material (~$2.4M combined) and discussable levers for Year-2 — without claiming this is the Interpretive cost engine.
