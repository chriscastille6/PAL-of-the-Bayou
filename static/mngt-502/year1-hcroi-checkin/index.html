1. Refresher: HCCF & HCROI

Fitz-enz human capital metrics connect people costs to organizational financials. Cascio–Boudreau costing breaks the big cost drivers into measurable buckets you can discuss with your instructor.

HCCF — four components

  • Pay & benefits — wages/salaries plus benefits load
  • Contingents — temps, contractors, agency spend
  • Absenteeism $ — cost of scheduled time lost
  • Turnover $ — cost of separations and replacement

HCCF = Pay & benefits + Contingents + Absenteeism + Turnover

HCROI (Fitz-enz)

HCROI = (Revenue − (Expenses − HCCF)) / HCCF

Related metrics: HCRF = Revenue / Total FTEs; HCVA = adjusted profit / Total FTEs. Use the live calculator for the full set once your cost totals are ready.

Turnover cost buckets

Separation · Replacement · Training · Performance (ramp / lost productivity)

Rate: separations ÷ average workforce

Absenteeism cost buckets

Absentees · Managing absence · Substitutes · Reduced quantity/quality

Rate: days lost ÷ (workforce × workable days)
