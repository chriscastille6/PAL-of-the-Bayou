South Louisiana / Acadiana ACS chart — source notes
========================================================

Dataset: ACS 2024 5-year (2020-2024)
Release id: acs2024_5yr
Publisher: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey
Access path: Census Reporter API (https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr)

Why not api.census.gov?
  Requests without a key redirect to missing_key.html.
  www.census.gov / QuickFacts often return Cloudflare blocks here.
  Census Reporter redistributes the same ACS estimates publicly.

Geography:
  Values are parish (county-equivalent) estimates. There is no Census Bureau ‘Acadiana’ summary geography on this chart; Lafayette / Lafourche / Terrebonne are shown as south Louisiana anchors (Acadiana + Houma–Thibodaux).

Method:
  % aged 65+ = sum of B01001 male and female age cells 65+ divided by total population (B01001001), rounded to 1 decimal.

Tables:
  B01001: Sex by Age — used for % aged 65+
  B03002: Hispanic or Latino Origin by Race — composition context
  B23025: Employment Status for the Population 16 Years and Over — LFPR
