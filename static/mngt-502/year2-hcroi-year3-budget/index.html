Do not pitch HCROI alone

HCROI is one language for the ask. Short-term HCROI can trade off against advantage in the talent marketplace (Wright, 2008; HPWS / fit). Cutting training or programs to inflate the ratio can make you worse at hiring, keeping, and developing people.
