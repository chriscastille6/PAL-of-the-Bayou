Utility Analysis · Fisher & Connelly (2017)

Part-time / contingent worker utility

Compare annual net value of permanent employees, independent contractors (direct or agent-of-record), and temporary workers. Defaults follow Fisher and Connelly (2017). Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Net value = service value − (wages + benefits + training + turnover). Classroom model only — not legal, tax, or staffing advice.
