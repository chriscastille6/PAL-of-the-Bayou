Short list of public teaching and research tools. These are not institutional compliance systems.

### [AI Disclosure Form](/ai-disclosure/)

Document AI tool use on an assignment, then print or save a PDF. No data is collected on this site.

### [Assessment Platform](/platform/)

Browse the public assessments hub (sample reports and soft-fail launches). Not the auth-gated catalog.

### [Correlation Calibrator](/correlation-calibrator/)

Calibrate intuition about correlation strength with published anchors and a BESD view.

### [HR SJT Modular Surveys](/hr-sjt-assessment/modular-surveys/)

Eight pre-decision rating packs for HR Analytics. Complete a pack in the browser, download your PDF, then upload it to Canvas. No account and no zip required.

### [Program ROI / Financial Analysis](/platform/tools/roi-calculator/)

Evaluate unique HR programs with hurdle rates, NPV, and IRR (Director-style financial analysis; simulation program costs). Not organization-level Fitz-enz HCROI. Synthetic classroom examples only; nothing is uploaded or stored.

### [Human Capital ROI (Fitz-enz org metrics)](/platform/tools/hcroi-calculator/)

Estimate organization-level Fitz-enz human capital metrics (HCROI, HCVA, HCCF, and related) from financials and workforce costs. Distinct from program-level NPV/IRR analysis above.

### [HRIS Desk Tour (teaching mock)](/platform/tools/hris-demo/)

Click through a static HRIS-style desk (Employees, Attendance, Leave, Recruitment, Performance) with synthetic records. Classroom showcase only — not a live production system.

### [Adverse Impact Analyzer](/platform/tools/adverse-impact/)

Primary classroom screen: firm composition vs community availability (χ² goodness-of-fit), with a guided chi-square walkthrough. Follow-on hiring-funnel mode covers 4/5ths and educational Bayes factors (Courey & Oswald, 2024). Synthetic counts only — not legal advice; not UGESP certification.

### [Utility Analysis (UA)](/ua/home/)

BayouPAL-hosted interactive tools for training, staffing, and business case analysis. Open the Utility Analysis apps portal to choose a calculator.
